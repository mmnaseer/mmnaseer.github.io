About my research

I'm an early career researcher, specializing in corporate governance, ESG, and climate risk. My research examines how climate disclosure affects stock market volatility, how corporate culture influences earnings management, and the role of intangible liabilities in financial reporting.

My work sits at the intersection of finance, governance, and sustainability—exploring practical solutions to real-world problems in financial markets and corporate disclosure.
